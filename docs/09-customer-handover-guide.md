# 09 — Customer handover guide (plain English)

Your current website stays exactly as it is until you say go. We have built a new *unpublished* version that you can preview.

## What you can change without a developer
Open **Online Store → Themes → Customise** on the new theme.
- **Homepage:** click any section to change wording, pictures, which products or collection it shows, and the order of sections. Add or remove sections with "Add section".
- **Top banner message:** Header group → Announcement bar.
- **FAQ:** open the FAQ section (home) or the FAQ page and add, edit or reorder questions.
- **Colours, logo, social links, email/WhatsApp:** Theme settings (the paint-brush/cog icon).
- **Your details (address, phone, VAT number):** Theme settings → Business details. Leave blank anything you don't want shown.

## Adding a new scent
1. Products → Add product. Title, description, price, photos (portrait 3:4 looks best).
2. Fill the **Scent family**, **Top/Heart/Base notes** and **Inspired by** boxes (at the bottom of the product page) — these create the notes display and the scent filter.
3. Add it to the *Wax Melts* collection. It appears on the website by itself.
4. Out of stock? Set inventory to 0 — the site shows "Sold out" and blocks the button automatically.

## Bundles and special products
- **Bundle deal:** works exactly as before (pick a pair of scents). To change the pairs, edit the product's variants. Please fix the spelling "Crimsion" when you do.
- **Request a specific scent:** customers type the scent they want on the product page; it appears on your order.

## Things only you can do (we cannot decide these for you)
Policies (terms, returns, shipping, privacy), delivery prices and times, cookie banner, your business name/address, product safety wording, newsletter tool. See `docs/06` and `docs/08`.

## If something looks wrong
Themes → find your old theme → Publish. That puts the old site back instantly; no orders or customers are affected.
