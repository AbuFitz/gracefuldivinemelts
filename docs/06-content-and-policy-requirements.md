# 06 — Content and policy requirements

**Nothing here is legal advice.** Policy wording must be reviewed by the business owner (and a qualified adviser where marked). The theme never invents business identity, prices, delivery terms, returns address, VAT status or product-safety data.

## Drafting stance
Policy *text* is **not** shipped as final. The Delivery & returns page renders the owner's Shopify records and shows a visible placeholder where a record is missing. Existing live returns wording is reused verbatim (with "This does not affect your statutory rights." added) in the FAQ and Delivery & returns summary; it is flagged above for review rather than silently rewritten.

## Product-safety and labelling items to verify (not asserted by the theme)
- Whether each fragranced wax melt needs CLP labelling and allergen declarations (trade guides disagree; check HSE/Trading Standards/supplier SDS).
- Pack contents, nominal weight and cell count per product (not present in any current copy).
- Wax/fragrance supplier details; batch/manufacturer identity.
- Burner/warmer guidance (wattage 14–25 W is current store copy, unverified).
- Comparative claims naming third-party perfumes: current disclaimer is retained; have the wording reviewed.
