### Built to work properly

- **Phone first.** Designed and checked on small phones, large phones, tablets and desktop. Pages do not scroll sideways, buttons are easy to tap and the added-to-bag message fits.
- **Accessible.** Works with a keyboard, screen readers and "reduced motion" settings. Animations switch off for people who need that.
- **Fast.** Optimised images, nothing heavy loaded up front, no third-party animation libraries.
- **Your content stays yours.** Products, prices, stock, orders, customers and policies all stay in your Shopify. Nothing is rebuilt or re-entered by hand.
